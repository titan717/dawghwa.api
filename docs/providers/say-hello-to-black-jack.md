# Say Hello to Black Jack (candidate: author-licensed manga, self-hosted)

Checked 2026-11-23 by SCOUT (cycle 0). Desk research only. Nothing was fetched from the rights holder's servers, and no files were downloaded.

## What it is

The original 13-volume series by Shuho Sato (not the sequel and not his other works). Since 2012-09-15 the author and his company have allowed free secondary use, commercial or not, with no prior contact needed. The company distributes the official PDF data for all 13 volumes for free. This would not be a scraped provider. It would be content we host ourselves from the official data.

## Terms summary (own words)

- Allowed: reproducing and publicly transmitting the work for free, including commercially; translations and adaptations. The rights holder won't enforce copyright or the moral right of integrity against these uses.
- Conditions: only the company's own data for volumes 1 to 13 may be redistributed (not third-party scans or re-layouts). Each use must **show the title, the author name and the site name ("Manga on Web")** exactly as specified. Use on illegal sites, and political uses that imply endorsement, are forbidden. A post-use report to the company is requested within one month. Rights clearance for a few quoted song lyrics (JASRAC) is the user's responsibility.
